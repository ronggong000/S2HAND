S2HAND is derived from Meta's Sapiens2 models and preserves their 308-keypoint
whole-body pose interface. Sapiens2 code, model weights, and derivatives are
subject to the Sapiens2 License included in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Users must
review and comply with that license, including its use restrictions.

Official Sapiens2 base checkpoints are not redistributed by this repository.
The loader downloads the exact official base associated with each S2HAND delta
from Meta's Hugging Face repositories.
